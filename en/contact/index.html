Getting there

The hospital is in the Yüreğir district of Adana, in Tahsilli neighbourhood, on Kozan Avenue (No:321). The “Get directions” button opens directions from your location to the hospital in your map app.

Opening hours

For current opening hours, please ask at the hospital switchboard.

Emergency

For urgent symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, loss of consciousness, sudden weakness or bleeding that does not stop, call 112 without waiting.
