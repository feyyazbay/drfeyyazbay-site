About

Dr. Feyyaz Bay is an internal medicine specialist working at Özel Altınkoza Hastanesi in Adana. This site explains the organ systems as one connected whole.

Internal medicine is the branch of medicine that takes a whole-person view of the diagnosis and follow-up of adults across many organ systems, such as heart and blood vessels, lungs, digestion, liver, kidneys, hormones and blood. The information on this site is for general information only; it does not replace an examination or treatment.
